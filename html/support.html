Frequently Asked Questions

How does AstroPair work?
AstroPair uses astrology to match you with compatible partners. We analyze birth charts to find relationships with strong astrological compatibility.
How do I update my birth information?
Go to your profile settings and tap "Edit Profile". You'll find options to update your birth date, time, and location.
What is AstroPair Plus membership?
AstroPair Plus gives you unlimited matches, advanced filtering, priority visibility, and detailed astrological insights for better compatibility matching.
How do I cancel my subscription?
You can manage your subscription through the App Store (iOS) or Google Play Store (Android). Go to your account settings in the respective store.
Why can't I see matches?
Make sure your birth information is complete and accurate. Also check that your location permissions are enabled for better matching.
How do I report inappropriate content?
Use the report button on any profile or message. Our team reviews all reports within 24 hours.

Account Issues

I forgot my password
Use the "Forgot Password" link on the login screen. We'll send reset instructions to your email.
How do I delete my account?
Go to Settings > Account Management > Delete Account. Note that this action is permanent.
