# From Code to Cosmos: The Birth of AstroPair
# 从代码到星空：AstroPair的诞生

## English Version

As a social product developer for many years, I've always been pondering: How can we make human connections more meaningful? Then came the day when I encountered astrology charts.

When I first saw that mysterious circular chart filled with celestial symbols, I was captivated. Those intricate relationships between constellations, those unique planetary aspects, seemed to tell the unique story inherent in each person. While studying astrology charts, I had an epiphany: Isn't this the answer I've been searching for?

The astrology chart, this crystallization of wisdom that has existed for thousands of years, is not just a tool used by astrologers to predict the future, but a key to understanding one's inner world. What if we could bring this depth of personal insight into social products? Wouldn't that make human connections more profound?

That's how AstroPair was born.

We didn't simply add zodiac matching to social features; we truly integrated astrological wisdom into every interaction. Through detailed chart analysis, we help users better understand themselves and others. Each match is not just about physical attraction, but about spiritual resonance.

At AstroPair, we believe:
• True connections should be built on deep understanding
• Everyone deserves to meet someone who can read their soul
• The stars guide not only sailors but also those seeking true love

This is not just a dating app, but a journey of self-discovery and love under the stars.

Welcome to AstroPair, let's find your star together in the cosmic sky.
